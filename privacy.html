Privacy Policy

Klar processes your audio on your Mac. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short

  • Klar does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • All audio processing happens on your device, in real time, in memory.
  • Klar does not connect to the internet. It does not upload audio, settings or usage data.
  • Your settings stay on your Mac. You can delete them at any time.

1. Who we are

Klar (“the app”) is developed and published by Emre Can Ayar (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the Klar app for macOS and to this support website.

2. Information the app accesses

Microphone

With your permission, Klar reads your microphone to remove noise, echo and other problems from your voice, and sends the processed audio to the output device you choose. The audio is processed in memory as it arrives and is discarded right after. It is not recorded, stored, transcribed or analysed for content.

System audio (audio from other apps)

With your permission (System Audio Recording), Klar captures the sound other apps play — for example the person you hear on a call — to clarify it and to use it as the echo-cancellation reference. This audio is also processed in memory only and is never stored or transmitted.

Which app is using the microphone

If you turn on automatic start with calls or per-app presets, Klar checks which app (for example Zoom or a browser) is currently using the microphone, using its bundle identifier. Klar does not read the content of your calls, windows or browser tabs, and does not keep a history of the apps you use.

Test room recordings

In the Test room you can record a short sample of your voice to compare settings. The sample is kept in memory only and is gone when you record a new one or quit Klar. Only if you choose Save before and after does Klar write two WAV files, to the folder you select. Those files are yours; Klar does not read or send them anywhere. The same applies to any image you save from Klar: it goes only where you choose.

Files you import

If you import a custom noise-reduction model, Klar copies that file into ~/Library/Application Support/Klar on your Mac so it can use it later.

3. Information stored on your Mac

To remember your choices, Klar stores the following locally in its preferences (com.klar.voice) on your Mac:

  • Effect settings, built-in and saved presets, and per-app preset rules.
  • Selected input and output devices, keyboard shortcuts, interface language and similar options.
  • A daily summary for the Calls screen: how many minutes Klar processed today and the average amount of noise removed. It contains no audio and resets every day.

This data never leaves your Mac through Klar. It may be included in your own Mac backups (for example Time Machine), which are under your control.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or store any personal data, audio, device identifiers, usage data, diagnostics or location.
  • We do not sell or share data, and we do not use data for advertising or tracking across apps and websites.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party data-collecting SDKs.
  • We do not use your audio to train machine-learning models. The noise-reduction models in Klar run locally and do not learn from your audio.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

5. Third parties

Klar does not send data to any third party. A few things outside the app are run by other companies under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple — if you download Klar from the App Store, Apple handles the purchase and download and may provide us aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports only if you have chosen to share them with developers in your Mac settings. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and Focus — Klar’s actions are handled by macOS on your device according to Apple’s policies.
  • Links — Klar may open a link (for example the BlackHole website) in your browser. Websites you visit have their own policies.
  • GitHub — this website is hosted on GitHub Pages and support requests use GitHub Issues. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to serve the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Support requests you post are public.

6. Permissions and your choices

You can allow or revoke Klar’s access at any time:

  • Microphone: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  • System audio: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
  • Open at login: System Settings → General → Login Items.

If you revoke a permission, the related feature stops working; the rest of the app keeps working.

7. Data retention and deletion

Audio is never retained. Settings stay on your Mac until you delete them. To remove everything Klar stored, quit Klar and run in Terminal:

defaults delete com.klar.voice

rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/Klar

Deleting the app and these files removes all Klar data. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side; you can still contact us with any request.

8. Children

Klar is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since Klar does not collect personal data, these rights are met by default. You can contact us at any time with questions.

10. Security

Klar is signed and notarized by Apple and runs with the hardened runtime. Because audio never leaves your device, there is no transmission or server storage to protect.

11. Changes to this policy

If the way Klar handles information changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes. The full history of this page is public in the site repository.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open a request at github.com/emrecanayar/klar-support.
