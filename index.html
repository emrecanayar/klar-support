Klar Support

Klar cleans your microphone and clarifies the audio you hear, so calls stay clear in a noisy room.

Requirements

  • A Mac running macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
  • A microphone (built-in, USB or Bluetooth headset).
  • Optional: a virtual audio device such as BlackHole, so other apps can pick up Klar’s cleaned microphone.

Klar works fully offline. It does not need an internet connection or an account.

Getting started

  1. Open Klar. The welcome screen walks you through the first setup.
  2. Allow Microphone and System Audio Recording access when macOS asks (see Permissions).
  3. Under Audio chains, open a chain and click Start. Klar has two chains:
    • Your voice — cleans your microphone; the other person hears this audio.
    • The other side — clarifies incoming audio and evens out its level.
  4. Not sure which preset fits? Click Set up this room: Klar listens to the room for a moment and suggests one. Use Compare to briefly hear the raw audio.
  5. Klar keeps running when you close the window. Use the waveform icon in the menu bar to stop it or open it again.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Klar asksWhere to change it
MicrophoneTo process your voice in real time so it stays clear on calls.System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone
System Audio RecordingTo clarify the audio other apps play (the person you are listening to) and to use it as the echo-cancellation reference.System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording
Login Items (optional)Only if you turn on Open at login.System Settings → General → Login Items

Audio is processed in memory on your Mac and is never recorded to disk, unless you explicitly export a clip from the Test room. See the Privacy Policy.

Using Klar in calls (Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, Slack, FaceTime)

  1. Install a virtual audio device such as BlackHole 2ch. Call apps can only pick up Klar through a virtual device.
  2. In Your voice → Devices, choose your real microphone as Microphone and BlackHole 2ch as Output (it is marked “virtual”). Click Start.
  3. In Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord or Slack, choose BlackHole 2ch as the microphone.
  4. Without a virtual device, the other person keeps hearing your Mac’s own microphone; Klar can still clarify the audio you hear, which needs no driver.
  5. Mute and push-to-talk reach the call only when the call app uses Klar’s virtual output as its microphone.

Listening (The other side)

While listening is on, the sound of other apps passes through Klar and the original is turned down, so you hear only the clarified version. When you turn it off, system audio goes back to normal.

With Listen to you choose what passes through the chain: every app (music and notifications included), only the call app that is using the microphone, or one specific call app. Choose where Klar plays under Speaker / headphones.

Presets and the effect chain

Built-in presets: Call, Meeting, Podcast, Radio voice, Stream & game, Noisy place, Movies & TV, Laptop speakers, Vivid, Music and Flat. You can save your own presets too.

Each chain is built from effects you can turn on, reorder and fine-tune, including echo cancellation, noise reduction, noise floor, de-reverb, de-click, rumble filter, gate, expander, equalizer, bass, de-esser, pitch, compressor, auto gain, reverb, maximizer, limiter and de-clip.

Advanced: in Noise reduction you can import your own RNNoise model file. Klar copies it into its own folder on your Mac.

Automation, shortcuts and Siri

  • Start with calls: Klar can turn itself on when a call app you pick (Zoom, browser/Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Discord, Slack) starts using the microphone, and turn off when the call ends. Klar only checks which app is using the microphone; it does not read the call.
  • App presets: switch presets automatically depending on the app in use.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: set global shortcuts for mute and push-to-talk. Klar warns you if a shortcut is already used by macOS.
  • Siri, Spotlight and Shortcuts: say or run “Start Klar”, “Stop Klar”, “Mute Klar” or “Switch Klar to <preset>”.
  • Focus filter: attach a Klar preset to a Focus mode in System Settings → Focus.
  • Open at login: turn on in Klar’s settings.

Test room

The Test room lets you hear the effect chain before a call. Play built-in noise scenes (café, construction, dog, fan, keyboard) mixed with speech, or record a short sample of your own voice, and compare before/after.

Your sample stays in memory. If you choose Save before and after, Klar writes two short WAV files (original and processed) only to the folder you pick.

Troubleshooting

The other person still hears background noise.

If Klar shows “Not reaching calls”, follow Using Klar in calls. Make sure your call app’s microphone is set to the device Klar outputs to (a virtual device such as BlackHole). If the call app uses your Mac’s built-in microphone directly, Klar cannot clean it. Also turn off the call app’s own noise suppression if it makes your voice sound choppy.

Klar does not hear my microphone / the level meter stays flat.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure Klar is on. Then check that the right input device is selected in Klar. Quit and reopen Klar after changing the permission.

Listening does nothing, or I hear the audio twice.

Allow Klar under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, then restart listening. If you hear audio twice, check that Klar’s output and the app’s output are not routed to two different devices.

There is an echo on calls.

Use headphones if you can. Otherwise keep Echo cancellation on in Your voice and allow System Audio Recording so Klar knows what your speakers are playing.

My voice sounds robotic or cut off.

Lower the Noise reduction strength or the Gate threshold, or try the Meeting or Flat preset. Very strong settings can remove quiet parts of speech.

A keyboard shortcut does not work.

The shortcut may already be used by macOS or another app. Choose a different combination in Klar’s settings.

Klar uses too much CPU.

Turn off effects you do not need, especially heavy ones such as De-reverb or neural noise reduction on both chains at the same time.

Reset and uninstall

  1. Quit Klar from the menu bar icon.
  2. Drag Klar from the Applications folder to the Trash.
  3. Optional, to remove settings and imported models, run in Terminal:

    defaults delete com.klar.voice

    rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/Klar

  4. Optional: remove Klar from System Settings → Privacy & Security (Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording) and from Login Items.
